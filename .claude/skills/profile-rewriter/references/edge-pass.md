# Edge pass

For each apparent weakness, identify the adjacent strength a hiring manager can believe:

| First concern | Credible flip | Proof needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [e.g., not enough management tenure] | [e.g., repeatedly led cross-functional delivery] | [specific story] |

Then test the profile: does its top third make the relevant strength legible without denying the
concern or overselling it? Rewrite around one coherent edge, not a defensive FAQ.
