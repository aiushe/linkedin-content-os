# About patterns

Only the first three lines are visible before "see more." Open with a concrete, relevant
positioning statement, not a résumé summary. Make scanning effortless with short sections,
white space, and simple markers where they fit the user's voice.

Offer three variants: direct/outcome-first, story-first, and specialist-first. Include target
keywords naturally; never turn the About section into a keyword list. Address a real red flag
briefly and confidently when the corpus supports it.
