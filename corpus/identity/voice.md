# Voice profile

This is a living constraint file. It starts empty on purpose: derive its fingerprint from real
samples with `pipeline/voice.py fingerprint`, then promote edit-diff rules only after a pattern
appears at least three times.

## Global rules

- Write with concrete scenes, direct verbs, and the user's actual vocabulary.
- Do not manufacture precision, testimonials, outcomes, or certainty.
- Prefer the user's real rhythm over generic LinkedIn formatting.

## Starting banned tells

- `delve`
- `leverage` when `use` says the same thing
- `it's not X, it's Y`
- rhetorical-question openers
- artificial three-item lists
- `here's the thing`

## Mode notes

### Posts

Use first-person experience and teach from what happened. Authority posts explain how I did it;
reach posts may use broader how-to framing.

### Comments

Be specific and useful, then leave an open question when a real conversation is possible.

### Profile copy

Optimize for instant comprehension and credible outcomes, not cleverness.

### DMs

Keep them short, personal, and easy to decline.
